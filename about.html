About Me

I'm Prince Sackey, an AI & Data Science practitioner with a background in Mathematics Education (QTS). I enjoy building intelligent systems, analysing data, and applying machine learning to real problems — usually starting from the same first-principles approach I used to teach a proof.

I'm currently based in Hull, United Kingdom, completing my MSc in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science.

How I work

Years in the classroom taught me to distrust an answer I can't explain simply. I bring that habit into data work: checking assumptions before modelling, being explicit about what a metric does and doesn't show, and writing up results so a non-specialist could follow the reasoning, not just the conclusion.
