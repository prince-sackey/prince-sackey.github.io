Vehicle Damage Classification

Python · TensorFlow/Keras · MobileNetV2 · Streamlit · Gradio

A 6-class image classifier for insurance claim triage — crack, dent, glass shatter, lamp broken, scratch, or tire flat — comparing a custom CNN baseline against MobileNetV2 transfer learning. Reached 84% test accuracy, with a full confusion matrix and per-class breakdown showing exactly where it's reliable and where it isn't.
