Applied Work

My deployed projects serve as practical demonstrations of my research interests. The vehicle damage classifier explores model evaluation under real‑world noise, comparing a custom CNN with MobileNetV2 transfer learning. The SMS spam detector examines class imbalance and metric selection, showing how accuracy can fail while recall and F1 reveal the true behaviour of a model. Both projects include full evaluation write‑ups that connect directly to my focus on fairness, reliability, and responsible deployment.
